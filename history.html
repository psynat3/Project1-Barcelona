A short timeline

  1. 1899: The club is founded in Barcelona.
  2. 1957: Camp Nou opens as the club's new home stadium.
  3. 1979: The club wins its first European trophy, the Cup Winners' Cup.
  4. 1992: The club wins its first European Cup/Champions League title.
  5. 2009: The club wins a historic treble: La Liga, Copa del Rey, and the Champions League.

Major trophies

La Liga
Won multiple times across the club's history, most recently in the current decade.
Copa del Rey
The club's domestic cup, won more times than any other club in the competition's history.
UEFA Champions League
Won five times, most recently in 2015.
