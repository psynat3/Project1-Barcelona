Welcome

Culers Corner is my personal fan site tracking the squad, the fixtures, and the club's history. Built for CIT 384, styled by hand, no frameworks involved.

A short highlights clip from this season.

Why this club

Barca's academy, La Masia, has produced generation after generation of players raised on the same style: quick passing, tight spacing, and patience on the ball. Following the club means following that idea as much as any one season's results.

Around the site

  • Squad: current players, numbers, and positions.
  • Matches: upcoming fixtures and recent results.
  • History: a short timeline and the trophy case.

Culers Corner is an unofficial, student-built fan project. Not affiliated with FC Barcelona.
